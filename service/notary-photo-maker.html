Photo sizes that follow your form

Choose from common portrait dimensions, or enter a custom width and height in millimetres. You can download one photo or repeat it on an A4 sheet with adjustable margins and cutting guides.

The state list covers all 28 states and 8 Union Territories. Selecting a state is a reference for your work; it does not automatically assign a government-approved photo size. Use the dimensions on your form or those provided by the office receiving it.

Resize a photo for notary or affidavit paperwork

Begin with the dimensions supplied on your form or by the receiving office. This editor lets you prepare the photo at those dimensions; it does not certify the image or decide whether a document will be accepted. State selection is only a reference and does not apply an official photo-size rule.

Use a custom photo size in millimetres

Choose a preset such as 35 × 45 mm, or select Custom size and enter width and height. Multiply centimetres by ten: 3.5 × 4.5 cm becomes 35 × 45 mm. Inspect the face placement after changing size because a different frame proportion can change the crop.

Print affidavit photo copies on A4

Choose a sheet layout when you need multiple small photos. Copy capacity follows the selected dimensions, margins and gaps. Download PDF for the paper layout, print at Actual size and measure a sample before using the rest. Background removal and skin retouch are optional; check the requirements for the intended document before altering it.

Uploads and privacy

JPG, PNG and WebP uploads are supported, up to 20 MB each. Large images are resized to at most 3200 pixels on the longest edge, and memory limits may reduce them further. Photo adjustments and print layout run on your device. Background removal and optional AI processing upload images to our photo service. If background removal is unavailable, the MODNet portrait matting model downloads from Hugging Face, with its JavaScript runtime from jsDelivr, and processes the photo on your device. Its edge quality can differ from the server result. Free limits use a signed browser cookie, hashed IP address and photo digests; the quota records do not store photos or raw IP addresses. This tool does not request saving photos for training.

Notary Photo — common questions

Does each state have a preset official size here?

No. The listed sizes are general options, not verified standards for every notary or department. Select the size required for your particular document.

How do I enter a size given in centimetres?

Choose Custom size and enter millimetres. For example, a 3.5 × 4.5 cm photo is 35 × 45 mm. The preview shows the final dimensions.

Can I print several small photos on one sheet?

Yes. Choose Copies on A4 and select a count or Fill sheet. Margins and gaps determine how many copies fit at the chosen size.
