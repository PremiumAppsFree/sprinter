Scan documents

Scan with your phone or computer camera, or add a file from your scanner. The page is found and straightened automatically. If it gets something wrong, drag the corners yourself. Then save a sharp PDF or JPG, at any size in KB.

1Scan or add pages

Scanning on a printer-scanner (HP, Canon, Epson)?

Scan in HP Smart or Windows Scan at 300 dpi (600 dpi for small text), save as JPG / PNG / PDF, then add the file here. It is cropped and cleaned the same way, and you can make it smaller below. A web page can’t start the scanner itself; your computer’s scan app has to do that part.

Everything happens on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
