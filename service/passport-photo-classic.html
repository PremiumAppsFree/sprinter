A4 passport photo sheet maker for cyber cafés and print shops

Crop each customer photo, set its copy count and arrange different customers on one print-ready A4 sheet. S Printer also includes exact photo sizes, background tools, manual enhancement, margin control and gap control in one browser workflow.

  • Auto face crop

Which photo sizes are available?
Choose India 35×45 mm, USA 2×2 inch, Canada 50×70 mm, UK and Schengen presets, or enter a custom width and height.
What can I edit online?
Which print sheets are supported?
Arrange one or many passport photos on A4, A5, Letter, 4×6 inch or 5×7 inch sheets with adjustable margins and gaps.
Can I place 6 passport photos in one A4 row?
No. The default six-per-row preset is about 32×41.14 mm. Six photos that are each 35 mm wide would use the entire 210 mm A4 width before any gaps or margins. Use the separate 35×45 mm preset when exact dimensions matter.
Can a cyber café add multiple customers to one sheet?
Yes. Crop each customer photo, add it to the print queue, set a separate copy count and arrange different customers on the same A4 sheet.
What can these photos be used for?
Prepare photos for passports, visas, PAN or Aadhaar records, exam forms, job applications, resumes and other identity documents.

How to make and print a passport photo sheet

  1. 1

    Upload and crop

    Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP photo. Select 35×45 mm, 2×2 inch or a custom size. Check the face position and use manual crop when needed.

  2. 2

    Arrange photos on one page

    Select Add to Sheet. Upload another customer photo if needed, set the copies for each image and choose A4, 4×6, A5, Letter or custom paper. Review margins and spacing.

  3. 3

    Download JPG or print here

    Use Download JPG for a high-resolution sheet, or Print Sheet to open the browser print dialog without leaving the editor. Preview / PDF also saves a PDF at the selected paper size.

Choose photo dimensions before choosing the sheet layout

Photo size and paper size are different settings. The six-per-row preset is a compact print-shop layout; select the separate 35×45 mm option when your form requests that size.

Print at the selected size, with room to cut

The sheet export uses a 300-DPI pixel grid. Match your printer paper to the selected sheet and choose Actual size or 100% scale. Fit to page can change photo dimensions. Disable browser headers and footers, check the preview and measure one test print. JPG print software may need you to set the paper size manually; PDF preserves the selected page dimensions.

One sheet for multiple customer photos

For cyber cafes and print shops, the queue lets you combine different people, set individual copy counts and rotate photos before export. Adjust horizontal and vertical gaps to leave cutting space. If the queue exceeds one sheet, reduce the copies or use a larger paper size; the export will ask you to correct it instead of silently omitting photos.

Passport photo sheet questions

Can I download my passport photo sheet as JPG?

Yes. Add cropped photos to the sheet and click Download JPG below the live preview. Your queue stays on this page so you can change the copies or paper and export again.

Can I print without opening another page?

Yes. Print Sheet opens the browser print dialog from this page. Choose the same paper size as the editor and print at 100% scale.

Can I put different people on one A4 sheet?

Yes. Add each cropped photo to the queue, choose its copy count and review the combined layout before downloading or printing.

Are six photos per A4 row the same as 35×45 mm?

No. The default six-per-row preset is about 32×41.14 mm. Six photos that are each 35 mm wide would use the entire 210 mm A4 width before any gaps or margins. Use the separate 35×45 mm preset when exact dimensions matter.
