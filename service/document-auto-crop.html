Straighten WhatsApp documents and mobile photos for A4 printing

A document photographed at an angle can look tilted, with one side wider than the other. Upload the image saved from WhatsApp or your phone, place the four crop corners on the paper edges, and apply the crop to correct its perspective. Rotate sideways images before cropping and check small edges with the magnifier.

WhatsApp से आए टेढ़े document को सही करके print कैसे करें?

WhatsApp की document photo फोन या कंप्यूटर में save करें और ऊपर upload करें। ID card के लिए ID Card, पूरे कागज़ के लिए A4 Document और छोटी पर्ची या रसीद के लिए Custom Size चुनें। Manual crop में चारों dots को document के असली कोनों पर रखें, जरूरत हो तो rotate करें और Apply crop दबाएँ। Preview में आकार और किनारे जाँचकर PDF, JPG या A4 print तैयार करें।

Choose the right document shape

  • ID card: add the front and back, crop each side and arrange the pair on one A4 sheet.
  • A4 document: straighten a form, certificate or photographed page and fit it within the paper margins.
  • Custom Size: crop a receipt, slip or small paper and set its width and height independently in millimetres.

Make the print readable

Adjust brightness and contrast while checking the preview. Cropping removes unwanted background and perspective correction straightens the page; neither can reliably restore text lost to blur or compression. For the clearest result, use the original photo or PDF from the sender. When printing, choose A4 and Actual size / 100%, with browser headers and footers off.

Manual crop, rotation, size adjustments and sheet layout happen in your browser. Automatic edge detection sends a reduced preview to the crop service. Your images stay in the current tab, so keep it open until your work is saved. For other print layouts, visit ID Card Print or Photo Print Studio.

Frequently asked questions

WhatsApp se aaye tedhe document ko seedha karke print kaise kare?

Save the document image from WhatsApp, upload it here and choose ID Card, A4 Document or Custom Size. Rotate it upright, align the four crop corners, then check the A4 preview before downloading or printing.

मोबाइल से खींची टेढ़ी document photo को सीधा कैसे करें?

फोटो upload करें, Manual crop खोलें और चारों छोटे dots कागज़ के चार कोनों पर रखें। Magnifier से किनारे जाँचें और Apply crop करें। इससे तिरछा दिख रहा कागज़ सीधा आयताकार बनता है।

Can this fix a blurry WhatsApp document?

Crop and perspective correction straighten the page and remove surrounding background. Brightness and contrast can improve readability, but missing or heavily blurred text cannot be recovered reliably. Ask the sender for the original image or PDF when text is unclear.

How do I crop a receipt or small slip to a custom size?

Choose Custom Size and mark the four corners of the slip. Select the cropped copy on the A4 preview and enter its width and height in millimetres. Use the plus and minus controls to scale both dimensions together.

How do I crop an ID card or A4 document?

Choose ID Card or A4 Document, upload a JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF, and use Auto crop. Review the result before adding copies to the print sheet.

What happens when auto crop is unavailable or incorrect?

The original upload opens in a four-corner manual crop editor. Move the corners to the document edges and apply the crop. You can also re-crop an automatic result at any time.

Can I print the front and back of a card?

Yes. Enable Double side and select images in front, back order. Two images form one ID card pair; four form two pairs on separate A4 sheets. Front above back is the default layout; Compact rows fits more copies on a sheet. You can also upload the sides individually. A4 document sides use consecutive pages; printer duplex settings are controlled by your printer.

Does the tool add another page automatically?

Yes. Copies flow onto additional A4 pages when a sheet is full. The PDF contains every page; JPG downloads the currently selected page.

Are uploaded documents sent to a server?

Manual cropping and print layout run in your browser. Auto crop sends a reduced preview to the existing S Printer crop service. This page does not save uploads to browser storage.

How should I print at the correct size?

Use A4 paper and Actual size or 100% in the print dialog. Turn off browser headers and footers. Use a margin supported by your printer.

Does preview zoom change the printed size?

No. Preview zoom changes only your view of the A4 sheet. The Print size controls resize the selected copy on the exported sheet. Click either side to adjust its brightness or contrast; click blank paper to clear the selection.

Can I upload another document while a crop is running?

Yes. Choose Add more in the crop progress bar, or use the upload controls. New uploads join a queue and are processed in order. Download your work before closing or refreshing the tab.

Does manual cropping need an external library?

Image cropping, sheet layout and PDF/JPG export run in the browser without OpenCV or a CDN library. PDF import loads a reader from this website only when needed. Automatic edge detection still needs the crop service to be reachable.
