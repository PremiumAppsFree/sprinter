About the AI

The picture is enlarged with Real-ESRGAN, and faces are restored with RestoreFormer++ (Ultra clear) or GFPGAN v1.4 (Natural). All are free, open-source research models, the same kind many “4K” enhancer apps use. They run inside your browser, on the graphics card when it can (WebGPU) or on the processor. Your photo never leaves this device.

The AI is prepared in the background as soon as this page opens, and each photo is sized so it finishes in about a minute even without a graphics card.
